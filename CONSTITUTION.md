# DSS Universe Constitution

> This document defines the fundamental engineering principles of DSS Universe.
>
> These principles guide every architectural decision, development process, and long-term evolution of the project.

---

# Preamble

DSS Universe is built as a long-term software platform.

We believe that great software is not created by writing more code, but by building systems that remain understandable, maintainable, and scalable over time.

Every engineering decision should contribute to the long-term health of the project.

This constitution exists to protect those principles.

---

# Article I

## Build for years, not for weeks.

Short-term convenience must never compromise long-term maintainability.

Every feature should be designed with future evolution in mind.

---

# Article II

## Architecture before implementation.

Every significant feature begins with architecture.

Code is the implementation of a design, not the design itself.

---

# Article III

## Simplicity over cleverness.

Readable solutions are preferred over complex ones.

Engineering is not about writing impressive code.

Engineering is about solving problems clearly and reliably.

---

# Article IV

## Consistency over personal preference.

Shared standards are more valuable than individual coding styles.

Consistency reduces complexity and improves maintainability.

---

# Article V

## Documentation is part of the product.

Documentation is not optional.

A feature is not considered complete until its documentation reflects the implemented solution.

---

# Article VI

## Stable software over rapid delivery.

Shipping quickly is valuable.

Shipping reliable software is essential.

Quality is never sacrificed for speed.

---

# Article VII

## Every important decision must be explainable.

Architectural decisions should be documented.

Future contributors should understand not only what was built, but why it was built that way.

---

# Article VIII

## Git history is part of the architecture.

Commits, branches, releases, and tags form the historical record of the project.

A clean Git history is considered an engineering asset.

---

# Article IX

## Standards are mandatory.

Engineering standards exist to simplify collaboration.

They are followed consistently unless officially revised.

---

# Article X

## Knowledge is a project asset.

Engineering knowledge should never exist only in someone's memory.

Important knowledge belongs in documentation.

---

# Article XI

## Continuous improvement.

No architecture is perfect.

Every decision may evolve when a better solution is discovered.

Improvements should strengthen the project without compromising its stability.

---

# Closing Statement

Technology evolves.

Frameworks change.

Programming languages change.

Engineering principles endure.

This constitution exists to ensure that DSS Universe continues to evolve without losing its identity.

---

**Build for years, not for weeks.**
