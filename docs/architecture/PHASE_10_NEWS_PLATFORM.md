# Phase 10 — News Platform Architecture

> Status: Packages 10.0–10.5 implemented

## Product boundary

News is a moderated publication product, not a personal blog and not a generic
CMS. News owns articles, immutable revisions, editorial state, publication
dates and presentation policy. DSS Editor owns document validation and derived
projections. Media owns files. Interaction Platform owns comments, reactions
and bookmarks. Content Access owns reusable gates.

## Short and full contracts

One `NewsArticle` has two projections rather than duplicated records.

- Short News: cover, type, category, title, authored short text, author,
  publication date, views, comments, rating, bookmark and badges.
- Full News: the Short contract plus canonical document delivery, type-specific
  presentation, table of contents, reading metadata, attachments, sharing,
  related content and comments.

The authored short text is not generated by truncating the full document.

## Publication types

The frozen top-level types are `STANDARD`, `TEXT`, `GALLERY`, `VIDEO` and
`AUDIO`. Polls are structured document blocks rather than another article type.
Drafts may carry incomplete type data, while editorial submission enforces the
complete type contract. Gallery requires at least three Media identities;
Video and Audio accept either a DSS Media identity or an HTTPS source.

## Lifecycle

```text
DRAFT → IN_REVIEW → CHANGES_REQUESTED → APPROVED
                                      → SCHEDULED → PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED
```

Commands, not arbitrary field updates, own transitions. Authors submit only
their own complete drafts. `news.review` owns change requests and approval;
`news.publish` owns publication. Approval is pinned to an exact revision, so a
different revision can never be published under stale approval.

## Dates

- `createdAt` is server-owned and immutable.
- `submittedAt`, `approvedAt`, `publishedAt` and `archivedAt` record lifecycle.
- `displayPublishedAt` is the privileged editorial override.
- ordinary authors never select a creation or publication timestamp;
- privileged scheduling and backdating are audited commands delivered later.

## Content and revisions

The current article stores validated `NEWS` editor JSON plus plain-text and
search projections. Every accepted content state also appends an immutable
`NewsRevision`. Revision 1 is created atomically with the article. HTML is not
canonical input.

## Identity and interactions

Each article has one real FK-backed `InteractionTarget` of kind `news.article`.
The News owner policy fails closed unless the article exists and is published.
Comments and rating can be disabled per article. Interaction counters remain
rebuildable projections.

## Slugs and languages

Slugs are lowercase URL segments and unique per language. A later delivery
package adds redirect history for published slug changes. Language uses a
bounded BCP-47 subset such as `uk` or `en-US`.

## Events and evidence

Creation writes the article, initial revision, Interaction Target, Audit record
and `news.article.created.v1` Outbox event in one transaction. Payloads carry
identities and routing metadata, never the article body.

## Package 10.1 invariants

1. A draft starts at version 1 and has one immutable revision.
2. Article and revision use only a validated `NEWS` document.
3. `(language, slug)` is unique at the database boundary.
4. Every article owns exactly one immutable Interaction Target coordinate.
5. No article commits without Audit and Outbox evidence.
6. No shared interaction is authorized against an unpublished draft.

## Packages 10.2–10.3 delivery

- hierarchical categories with post-type and interaction defaults;
- one database-enforced primary category per article;
- normalized tags and many-to-many article coordinates;
- category/post-type scoped field definitions using bounded typed values;
- no arbitrary HTML custom fields;
- News-owned optimistic saves for `DRAFT` and `CHANGES_REQUESTED` states;
- canonical `NEWS` validation on every save;
- an immutable revision, Audit record and body-free Outbox event per accepted
  version;
- stale base versions fail without changing the article.

## Packages 10.4–10.5 delivery

- validated `STANDARD`, `TEXT`, `GALLERY`, `VIDEO` and `AUDIO` template data;
- permissive partial templates while drafting and strict readiness checks on
  editorial submission;
- author-owned submit from `DRAFT` and `CHANGES_REQUESTED`;
- permission-owned change requests, approval and publication;
- approval pinned to the exact immutable revision version;
- append-only editorial decisions with actor, revision, action, reason and
  timestamp;
- every accepted transition writes status, decision, Audit and a body-free
  Outbox event atomically;
- publication activates the existing Interaction Target owner policy without
  minting a second interaction identity.
