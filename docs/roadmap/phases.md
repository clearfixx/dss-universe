# Development Phases

> This document defines the major development phases of DSS Universe.
>
> Each phase represents a significant milestone in the evolution of the project.

---

# Purpose

The roadmap is divided into phases rather than dates.

A phase is completed only when its objectives are achieved.

This approach prioritizes quality and completed functionality over arbitrary deadlines.

# Future Phases

Additional phases will be introduced as the project evolves.

The roadmap is intended to remain flexible while preserving the project's long-term vision.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

The roadmap measures progress by completed work.

A phase is complete only when its objectives are fully implemented, documented, tested, and merged.

Progress is measured by quality, not by time.

---

**Build for years, not for weeks.**
